# VELUM — Launch Teaser · "This Winter"

**Format:** Instagram Story / Reel · 9:16 · 1080 × 1920 · 30 fps · 35–45 s · no dialogue
**Ambition:** a $20k agency-grade product film. The benchmark is Apple's iPad
films and the GoodNotes 6 launch — we are launching against GoodNotes and
intend to look more expensive than they do.

---

## 1 · What Velum is

Velum is a study app for iPad: **Canvas · Notes · Journal · Tasks ·
Flashcards** in one place, built around one idea — *everything connects*.
A lecture note becomes flashcards; the flashcard remembers the sentence it
came from; the task knows which note created it; the journal closes the day.
Ink on paper is the brand's material. The look is wintery, calm, editorial:
black ink, warm paper, one copper accent, a Times-like serif.

## 2 · The creative job

Make the product feel inevitable and expensive. The film's grammar is the
Apple device-film grammar:

- **Weight** — the iPad moves like it has mass (perspective entrances,
  settle-with-overshoot, motion blur on fast moves).
- **Depth** — three-quarter hero angles with a true vanishing point; UI
  layers lifting off the glass in parallax ("exploded UI") — but every
  lifted layer must be REAL UI from the provided material.
- **Closeness** — punch-ins where the screen fills the frame and the app's
  typography is readable.
- **One room** — a dark studio with directional winter light that runs
  through the whole film. Two live-action plates already exist in this
  workspace (see §5) and should bookend the film.
- **Claim first, proof second** — a caption line holds alone for a beat,
  then the UI proves it. Type never sits on top of the screen it describes.
- **One grade** — a single cool grade + fine 35 mm grain over everything.

Above all, the Apple rule: **clarity over spectacle.** Every move must show
something the app really does. Nothing may replace the product.

## 3 · Non-negotiables (brand guardrails)

1. **Real UI only.** Use the provided screen capture and stills. Never
   invent, redraw, retype, "clean up" or extrapolate UI. No lorem ipsum,
   no imaginary screens, no morphed interface elements. If a screen is
   needed that isn't in the material, ask — don't generate it.
2. **The wordmark is final art.** Use the provided PNG files as-is. Never
   redraw or restyle the logo, no glow, no bevel, no animation that
   distorts the letterforms. The copper full stop is part of the mark.
3. **Palette.** Ink `#1C1C1F` · paper `#F5F3EE` · copper accent
   `#D89B63` (on dark) / `#B5763F` (on light). No other saturated color.
   No gradients, no neon, no purple, no "AI blue".
4. **Typography.** Use the provided caption cards (transparent PNGs), or
   match them exactly: a transitional serif in the New York style,
   sentence case; small copper all-caps eyebrows with wide tracking.
5. **Tone.** Calm, precise, wintery, editorial. **Forbidden:** glossy 3D
   swooshes, particle explosions, lens flares, fake OS chrome, emoji,
   stock "student smiling at laptop" footage.
6. **Hands / Apple Pencil** are welcome ONLY if photoreal and flawless
   (correct finger count, correct pencil, correct screen content mirrored
   in the reflection). One imperfect hand costs more than it adds — when
   in doubt, no hands.
7. **Music:** sparse minor-key piano, slow, no build-up/drop. A 10 s piano
   sketch already exists in this workspace (task
   `cd935e08-1711-4a7e-9d4e-cfa0c4b9520a`) and may be extended.
8. **Language:** every visible word is English. The UI material is already
   fully English.
